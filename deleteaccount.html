Account Deletion – Quantix App

This page explains how users of the Quantix Physics Quiz App can request deletion of their account and associated data.

🗑 How to delete your account

You can request account deletion using any of the following methods:
  • Open the Quantix app → Settings → Delete Account
  • OR send an email request to: support@quantixapp.com

📊 Data that will be deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Firebase user profile
  • XP, scores, and leaderboard data
  • Quiz history and progress

⏳ Data retention

Data is permanently deleted within 7–30 days after verification of the request.

🔐 Security note

Some anonymized logs may be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance purposes.

📩 Contact

For any questions regarding data deletion, contact:
support@quantixapp.com
